Privacy Policy

PrimePicks · Effective 19 September 2026

PrimePicks is an advertiser in Uzbekistan that promotes products sold on the 100k.uz marketplace. This policy explains what information PrimePicks and its 100k-ads application handle, and why.

What the 100k-ads app is

100k-ads is an internal, server-side integration with the Meta Marketing API. PrimePicks uses it only to create, run and measure its own advertising campaigns on Facebook and Instagram, and to pass on orders that people place through our ad forms. The app:

  • does not offer Facebook Login and has no end users signing in to it;
  • is not offered to other businesses or the public;
  • does not read the profiles, posts or messages of Facebook or Instagram users — it receives only what a person chooses to submit in an order form on one of our ads.

Order forms on our ads

Some PrimePicks ads open an order form inside Facebook or Instagram. When you submit it, we receive the phone number shown in the form (you can edit it before submitting) together with the product you chose. The form asks for nothing else. We use them for one purpose: to place your order with 100k.uz, whose operator then phones you to confirm the order and the delivery address.

  • Your phone number is passed to 100k.uz straight away and is not stored by PrimePicks. We keep only a technical record that a form was forwarded (its form entry number, the time and the order status) so that an order is never sent twice.
  • Submitting the form is a request to be called about this order. Nothing is charged; you pay the courier only if you accept the parcel.
  • If you submitted a form by mistake, tell the operator when they call, or email us and we will ask 100k.uz to cancel the order.

Information we process

  • Advertising performance data for PrimePicks' own ad account — for example impressions, clicks, spend and budgets. Meta provides this in aggregate; it does not identify individual people.
  • Order status information from 100k.uz for orders placed through PrimePicks' links — the link identifier, order date and status (for example accepted, delivered, cancelled). We use it only to measure which ads lead to delivered orders.
  • Messages you send us, if you contact us, together with the contact details you choose to include.

Orders placed through our ads

Every order — whether placed through an ad form or on a 100k.uz product page — is placed with and fulfilled by 100k.uz. Your phone number, and the name and delivery address you give the operator, are held and processed by 100k.uz under its own privacy policy. On a 100k.uz product page you enter them directly with 100k.uz and the 100k-ads app never sees them.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not upload customer lists or contact details to Meta or any advertising platform.
  • We do not use information for any purpose other than running and measuring our own advertising.

Sharing

Information is shared only with the services needed to run our advertising: Meta Platforms (for Facebook and Instagram ads, including the order forms, which Meta hosts) and 100k.uz (which receives your phone number to fulfil the order). We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Retention and security

We keep advertising performance and order status data for as long as it is needed to measure campaigns, and no longer than 24 months. Access credentials are stored with restricted access and are never published.

Your rights and data deletion

You may ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Email navoiy498@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days. For order data held by 100k.uz, please also contact 100k.uz directly.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
